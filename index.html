The Problem

Drones are blind exactly where it matters most.

Every second of delay in a search-and-rescue operation compounds. Today's aerial platforms are built for open sky — not for the collapsed structure, the smoke-filled corridor, or the tunnel with no signal.

No GPS

Indoor, underground, and disaster environments have no satellite fix — most commercial autonomy stops here.

Unknown Environment

No prior map exists. The vehicle has to build its understanding of the space in real time.

Low Visibility

Smoke, dust, and darkness degrade the vision systems most drones depend on entirely.

Delay Costs Lives

In search and rescue, every additional minute of manual reconnaissance reduces survival odds.

Our Solution

One pipeline, from motor to mission.

A closed-loop autonomy stack that perceives, localizes, maps, plans, and reports — without ever touching a satellite signal.
